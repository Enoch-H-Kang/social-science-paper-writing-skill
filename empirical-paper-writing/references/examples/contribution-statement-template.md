# Contribution Statement Template (Empirical Papers)

Three short paragraphs closing the introduction. Findings say what is true; contributions say what the field can now do or know. Content is generic; replace brackets.

```latex
% Substantive: the question this paper settles or reframes
% Methodological: a capability the design or framework makes possible
% Managerial / policy: the guideline, the dollar value, or the regulatory implication
% Each paragraph ends with an "Unlike [closest work]" contrast
```

## Skeleton

1. **Substantive.** `To the literature on [market phenomenon], we provide [the first causal evidence / a settled answer] on [question]. Unlike [closest work], which [what it cannot do, stated respectfully], we [what this paper does].`
2. **Methodological.** `Our [design / framework] makes it possible to [capability], not merely to [weaker capability]. Unlike [closest approach], which [limitation], ours [difference].` State a capability, not a technique name.
3. **Managerial / policy.** `For [actor], our results imply [guideline with a number]: [concrete action] raises [outcome] by [X%] relative to [named baseline]. Unlike [closest work], we [counterfactual or incentive claim it could not make].`

## Rules

1. Do not restate findings in new words. If a sentence could sit in the findings preview unchanged, cut it.
2. Describe the closest work by what it cannot do, respectfully, and make the difference a question or capability rather than a resource.
3. Order paragraphs by audience: the literature the paper most belongs to first.

## Checklist

1. Does each paragraph state a capability or settled question rather than a finding?
2. Does each end with an "Unlike [closest work]" contrast that is precise and respectful?
3. Does the managerial paragraph carry a number and a named baseline?
4. Are the three paragraphs distinct in audience, with no overlap?
