# Conclusion Writing Guide (Empirical Papers)

## Goal

The conclusion states the paper's answer and its implications. It never introduces a new result.

## Structure

Use the relevant elements in the order that best carries the paper's answer.

1. **Compressed recap** of the findings in the fixed order (average, mechanism, heterogeneity, policy). It takes at most half the section.
2. **Managerial takeaway, when supported,** as a plain-language guideline: what to run, check, or stop doing ("evaluate a personalized policy offline against the best uniform one before deploying it").
3. **Policy or regulatory implication**, if one exists.
4. **Limitations and future directions.** State material boundaries explicitly; convert a limitation into a concrete next question when it opens one.
5. **Optional close** of one sentence on the broader question.

## Rules

1. Hedge in-text throughout the paper, attached to the claim being hedged, rather than quarantining a limitations dump here.
2. State scope conditions operationally: what a setting must have (randomized allocation, comparable user base, similar cost structure) for the result to transfer.
3. A substantive future direction may close when a specific limitation leads to a concrete next question.
4. Repeat a headline number when the paper has one, consistently with the abstract.

## Checklist

1. Does the recap follow the fixed order and stay within half the section?
2. Is any managerial guideline supported by the findings?
3. Are material limitations explicit, with concrete next questions where warranted?
4. Are scope conditions stated operationally?
5. Does the ending carry a supported payoff, implication, boundary, or concrete next question?
