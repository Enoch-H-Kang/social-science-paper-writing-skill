# Conclusion Writing Guide (Empirical Papers)

## Goal

The conclusion changes what a named actor will do. It never introduces a new result.

## Structure

1. **Compressed recap** of the findings in the fixed order (average, mechanism, heterogeneity, policy). It takes at most half the section.
2. **Managerial takeaway** as a plain-language guideline: what to run, check, or stop doing ("evaluate a personalized policy offline against the best uniform one before deploying it").
3. **Policy or regulatory implication**, if one exists.
4. **Limitations converted to future-work directions.** Each limitation names the direction it opens, never a bare confession.
5. **Optional close** of one sentence on the broader question.

## Rules

1. Hedge in-text throughout the paper, attached to the claim being hedged, rather than quarantining a limitations dump here.
2. State scope conditions operationally: what a setting must have (randomized allocation, comparable user base, similar cost structure) for the result to transfer.
3. Do not end on "future research should explore…". The final sentence belongs to the actor.
4. Repeat the headline number once, verbatim from the abstract.

## Checklist

1. Does the recap follow the fixed order and stay within half the section?
2. Is there a guideline an actor could run on Monday morning?
3. Is every limitation attached to a future direction?
4. Are scope conditions stated operationally?
5. Does the last sentence belong to an actor rather than to "future research"?
