# Conclusion Writing Guide (Method Papers)

## Goal

The conclusion states what the tool establishes and why it matters. It never introduces new results.

## Structure

Use the relevant elements in the order that best carries the paper's answer.

1. **Compact recap:** question, tool, findings.
2. **The substantive payoff, by track.**
   - Theory: the one-sentence formal result or capability a reader can repeat, plus an implication or extension when supported.
   - Structural: the guideline and its dollar value.
   - ML-method: a deployment paragraph covering where the tool pays off most, its cost, privacy and compatibility with existing systems, and code availability.
3. **Scope and generalizability conditions,** stated operationally: what a market or data setting must have for the result to transfer.
4. **Limitations and future directions.** State material boundaries explicitly; convert a limitation into a concrete next question when it opens one.

## Rules

1. No new results.
2. Hedge claims in the text where they are made rather than dumping hedges here.
3. A substantive future direction may close when it follows from a specific limitation or open question.
4. Repeat a headline number when the paper has one, consistently.

## Checklist

1. Is the recap compact (question, tool, findings)?
2. Is the actor payoff in the form the track calls for?
3. Are scope conditions operational?
4. Are material limitations explicit, with concrete next questions where warranted?
5. Does the ending carry a supported result, implication, boundary, or concrete next question?
