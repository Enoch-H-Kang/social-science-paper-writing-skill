# Literature and Positioning Guide (Method Papers)

## Goal

Place the paper at the intersection of two or three literatures, at least one substantive and at least one methodological, and make the gap a question none of them can answer, not a technique none used.

## Workflow

1. List the literatures, not the papers. Two or three, never five.
2. Write one paragraph per literature with the template below.
3. Close with the positioning sentence: what this paper does that needs the literatures combined.

## Paragraph Template (per literature)

1. Topic sentence naming the literature by its question.
2. What the stream established, in two or three findings.
3. What it assumes away or cannot compute (no dynamics, no selection, no equilibrium, no counterfactuals).
4. Transition: the missing piece is exactly one of this paper's challenges.

## Rules

1. **Position against questions, not against papers.** No "X (Year) did A; however, they did not do B" sequences.
2. **The closest paper gets the most respectful and precise treatment.** State its contribution in terms its author would accept, then the one axis of difference, as a question rather than a resource.
3. **Cite the canonical version.**
4. **No citation dumps.** More than three citations in a sentence means taxonomy, not argument.
5. **Cite method literatures for capability and failure modes.** Cite both the source of an estimator or algorithm and the paper that characterizes when it fails in settings like yours.
6. **Theory papers:** the positioning move is "existing models generate [pattern A] but cannot generate [the dichotomy we document]." The gap is a phenomenon the model must produce, stated before the model appears.
7. **Fence the novelty (ML-method papers).** Add a labeled Discussion paragraph naming the one or two closest algorithms or frameworks and the precise axis of difference.
8. **Structural variant.** Dismiss each stream on one precise structural axis (no dynamics, no selection, no equilibrium) and say why tools lacking that axis cannot answer the question.

## Checklist

1. Two or three literatures, each ending in a gap that names one of the paper's challenges?
2. Closest work treated precisely and respectfully, with the difference stated as a question?
3. Every claim about prior findings traceable to the cited paper's result?
4. Does the closing sentence require the combination, not either part alone?
5. Is the track-specific rule applied (phenomenon gap for theory, structural axis for structural, fenced novelty for ML)?
